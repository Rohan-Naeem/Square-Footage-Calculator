Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how the calculator works and how to get accurate square footage results.

What is square footage?

Square footage is a measurement of area expressed in square feet. It describes how much two-dimensional space a surface covers.

How do I calculate square footage?

For a rectangular area, multiply the length by the width. For example, an area that is 12 feet long and 10 feet wide has an area of 120 square feet.

What formula is used for a rectangle?

The basic rectangle area formula is: Area = Length × Width. Make sure both measurements use the same unit before calculating the area.

Can I calculate the area of a square?

Yes. The area of a square is calculated by multiplying its side length by itself: Area = Side × Side.

Can I calculate a triangle's area?

Yes. The area of a triangle can be calculated using Area = (Base × Height) ÷ 2. The base and height should use the same measurement unit.

Can I calculate the area of a circle?

Yes. A circle's area is calculated using Area = π × Radius². Enter the radius using the selected measurement unit.

Which measurement units does the calculator support?

The calculator supports feet, meters, inches, and yards. The selected unit is used for the measurements entered into the calculator.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

To convert square feet to square meters, multiply the square-foot value by approximately 0.09290304.

How do I convert square meters to square feet?

To convert square meters to square feet, multiply the square-meter value by approximately 10.7639.

Why do my square footage results need to be verified?

Calculator results depend on the measurements entered by the user. Incorrect measurements, rounding, unusual room shapes, or other real-world factors can affect the result.

Can I calculate an irregular-shaped area?

Irregular spaces can often be divided into smaller regular sections. Calculate each section separately and then add the areas together. Complex projects may require professional measurement.

Can I save multiple areas?

Yes. You can add calculated areas to the saved areas list and the calculator can display the combined square footage total.

Does the calculator store my measurements?

The calculator is designed to perform calculations in your browser. Review the Privacy Policy for information about website data handling and any third-party services.

Is the calculator suitable for professional projects?

The calculator is intended as a general calculation and informational tool. For construction, surveying, architectural work, purchasing decisions, or projects involving significant costs, measurements should be independently verified.
