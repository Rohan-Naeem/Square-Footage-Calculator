UNDERSTANDING AREA

Common Square Area Units

Area is measured in square units. Our calculator supports four common measurement units and automatically converts your calculated area into equivalent values.

FT²

Square Feet

Square feet (sq ft) are commonly used for measuring rooms, floors, homes, offices, and other spaces.

M²

Square Meters

Square meters (m²) are commonly used with the metric system for measuring areas and spaces.

YD²

Square Yards

Square yards (sq yd) are another unit used to measure surface area.

IN²

Square Inches

Square inches (sq in) are useful for smaller areas and detailed measurements.

CONVERSION FORMULAS

Common Area Conversion Formulas

These formulas explain how the calculator converts between the supported area units.

01

Square Feet to Square Meters

Square meters = square feet × 0.09290304

02

Square Feet to Square Yards

Square yards = square feet ÷ 9

03

Square Feet to Square Inches

Square inches = square feet × 144

EXAMPLES

Square Footage Conversion Examples

Example 1 · Square Feet to Square Meters
100 sq ft × 0.09290304 = 9.290304 m²
Example 2 · Square Feet to Square Yards
180 sq ft ÷ 9 = 20 sq yd
Example 3 · Square Feet to Square Inches
50 sq ft × 144 = 7,200 sq in
