How to Calculate Square Footage

Learn how to calculate square footage for rooms, floors, and common shapes using simple formulas, accurate measurements, and practical examples.

THE BASICS

What Is Square Footage?

Square footage is a measurement of area used to describe the size of rooms, floors, buildings, homes, and other spaces. It tells you how much two-dimensional surface area a space covers.

The formula used to calculate square footage depends on the shape of the space. For a rectangular room or floor, the basic calculation is length multiplied by width. Other shapes use their own area formulas.

You can also enter measurements in feet, meters, inches, or yards. The calculator converts the calculated area into square feet, square meters, square yards, and square inches.

Basic Square Footage Formula
Area = Length × Width

Example: 12 ft × 10 ft = 120 sq ft

01 · RECTANGLE

How to Calculate the Area of a Rectangle

A rectangle is one of the most common shapes for rooms, floors, offices, kitchens, bedrooms, and other spaces. This makes the rectangle formula the most common method for calculating square footage.

Measure the length and width of the space using the same measurement unit. Then multiply the two measurements to find the square footage.

Rectangle Formula
Area = Length × Width

Example: 15 ft × 12 ft = 180 sq ft

02 · SQUARE

How to Calculate the Area of a Square

A square has four equal sides. Because all sides are equal, you only need to measure one side to calculate its area.

Multiply the side length by itself to find the square footage of the space.

Square Formula
Area = Side × Side

Example: 10 ft × 10 ft = 100 sq ft

03 · TRIANGLE

How to Calculate the Area of a Triangle

To calculate the area of a triangle, measure its base and perpendicular height.

Multiply the base by the height and divide the result by two. This formula can be used when calculating the area of a triangular section of a larger space.

Triangle Formula
Area = (Base × Height) ÷ 2

Example: (12 ft × 8 ft) ÷ 2 = 48 sq ft

04 · CIRCLE

How to Calculate the Area of a Circle

For a circular space, measure the radius from the center of the circle to its outer edge.

The area is calculated using pi (π), which is approximately 3.14159. Make sure the radius and final area use the appropriate measurement units.

Circle Formula
Area = π × Radius²

Example: π × 5² ≈ 78.54 sq ft

MEASUREMENT UNITS

Supported Measurement Units

The calculator supports four common units for entering measurements. Always use the same unit for dimensions that are being multiplied together when calculating square footage.

FT

Feet (ft)

Enter measurements in feet when working with dimensions commonly used for rooms, floors, homes, and buildings.

M

Meters (m)

Use meters when your measurements are recorded using the metric system.

IN

Inches (in)

Inches can be useful for smaller areas or when dimensions are provided in inches.

YD

Yards (yd)

Use yards when your length and width measurements are provided in yards.

AREA CONVERSION

Square Footage Conversion

Once an area has been calculated, it can be expressed using different square-area units. Converting square footage is useful when measurements are provided in different unit systems or when a project requires another area measurement.

Common Area Conversions

1 sq ft = 0.092903 m²

1 sq ft = 0.111111 sq yd

1 sq ft = 144 sq in

IRREGULAR SPACES

How to Calculate Square Footage for an Irregular Area

Not every room or space has a simple rectangular shape. For an irregular area, divide the space into smaller sections that can be measured individually.

Calculate the area of each section and then add all the results together to find the total square footage. This method is useful for rooms, floors, yards, and other spaces with multiple sections.

Combined Area Formula
Total Area = Area 1 + Area 2 + Area 3
MULTIPLE AREAS

How to Calculate Total Square Footage for Multiple Rooms

If you need to calculate several rooms or spaces, calculate each area separately before combining the results.

On the calculator page, use the Add This Area button after calculating each space. Saved areas are displayed in a list and their square footage is combined into a grand total.

MEASUREMENT TIPS

Important Measurement Tips for Accurate Square Footage

  • Use the same measurement unit for dimensions being multiplied together.
  • Measure carefully instead of estimating whenever an accurate measurement is available.
  • For a triangle, use the perpendicular height with the base.
  • For a circle, measure the radius from the center to the edge.
  • Divide irregular spaces into smaller sections.
  • Calculate multiple rooms separately before combining their totals.
  • Double-check your measurements before using the result for project planning.
