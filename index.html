COMMON QUESTIONS

Square Footage Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate square footage?

For a rectangular space, multiply the length by the width using the same measurement unit.

Can I calculate square meters?

Yes. Select meters as the measurement unit and the calculator will display the area in square meters along with other conversions.

Can I calculate multiple rooms?

Yes. You can add individual areas and the calculator can keep a list of saved areas and calculate their combined total.
