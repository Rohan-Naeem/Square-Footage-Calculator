Square Footage Formula

Use a simple area formula based on the shape of the space you want to calculate.

01

Rectangle

Area = Length × Width. This is the basic formula for calculating square footage of most rectangular rooms and floors.

02

Square

Area = Side × Side. Multiply the side length by itself to calculate the square footage.

03

Triangle & Circle

Use the appropriate geometric formula for triangles and circles to calculate their total area.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Square Footage Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate square footage?

For a rectangular space, multiply the length by the width using the same measurement unit. The result is the square footage when measurements are given in feet.

Can I calculate square meters?

Yes. Select meters as the measurement unit and the calculator will display the area in square meters along with square feet, square yards, and square inches.

Can I calculate multiple rooms?

Yes. You can add individual rooms or areas and calculate their combined total square footage.
