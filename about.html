ABOUT THE TOOL

About Square Footage Calculator

A simple online resource designed to make everyday area calculations easier and clearer.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Area Calculations Simple

Square Footage Calculator is an online calculation tool designed to help users quickly determine the area of a space or surface from basic measurements.

Instead of performing the mathematical calculations manually, users can enter their measurements, select the appropriate shape and unit, and receive an area result along with common unit conversions.

The website is designed to be straightforward, responsive, and easy to use on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS

Area Formulas Used

The calculator uses standard mathematical formulas for each supported shape.

Rectangle

Area = Length × Width

Square

Area = Side × Side

Triangle

Area = (Base × Height) ÷ 2

Circle

Area = π × Radius²
IMPORTANT

Accuracy and Limitations

The calculator performs mathematical calculations based on the measurements entered by the user. Therefore, the accuracy of the result depends on the accuracy of those measurements.

Rounding, measuring errors, unusual shapes, and other real-world conditions may affect the final area.

For construction, surveying, architectural work, purchasing decisions, or other projects involving significant costs, measurements and calculations should be independently verified using appropriate professional methods.
